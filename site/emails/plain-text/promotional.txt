A PRACTICAL CONTENT SYSTEM FOR SMALL NONPROFIT TEAMS

Small nonprofit teams often need to keep several channels moving with limited time and capacity. A simple content system can make that work easier to plan, review, and reuse.

Join Lena Ortiz, Maya Chen, Theo Brooks, and Amina Patel for four connected sessions covering content planning, reuse, fundraising communications, and approvals.

The afternoon covers:
- Choose a useful set of recurring content themes
- Adapt one strong idea for several channels
- Connect content with fundraising moments
- Create a planning and approval rhythm your team can maintain
