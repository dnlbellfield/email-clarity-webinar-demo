How it works

A clear path from sign-up to follow-up

01

Simple sign-up

A short form asks only for an email address and an optional first name.

02

Confirmed permission

A confirmation email makes sure each person asked to receive the series before it begins.

03

Timely emails

Three connected messages arrive in the planned order, without someone sending each one by hand.

04

Privacy respected

Useful actions can be counted without passing along names, email addresses, or form answers.

Built around trust

Designed for the people signing up and the team running it

For the people signing up

  • They know exactly what they are requesting
  • They confirm their address before the series begins
  • They receive a short, connected set of useful emails
  • They can unsubscribe at any time

For the team running the campaign

  • Spam protection reduces fake sign-ups
  • Follow-up emails are sent automatically and in order
  • Existing contact preferences are respected
  • The full journey is checked in real inboxes
