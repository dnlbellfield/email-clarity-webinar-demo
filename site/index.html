Project status

What is ready and what still needs a final check

Ready and working

  • Mobile-friendly sign-up and confirmation pages
  • A short form asking only for an email address and optional first name
  • Clear permission and email-address confirmation
  • Secure spam protection before any request is accepted
  • Three connected emails prepared for automatic delivery
  • An unsubscribe option in every sequence email
  • Privacy-conscious activity tracking that avoids personal details

Final checks before public launch

  • Complete the controlled inbox test for all three emails
  • Confirm each follow-up arrives at the planned time
  • Confirm unsubscribing stops the remaining emails
  • Confirm unverified requests expire after 24 hours
  • Review the final privacy and data-retention wording
  • Move from limited testing to public availability
