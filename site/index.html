Built for both sides

Clear for people signing up and the team running it

For people signing up

  • They know what they’re requesting
  • They confirm before the series begins
  • They receive a short, connected set of emails
  • They can unsubscribe at any time

For the team running it

  • Spam protection reduces fake sign-ups
  • The email layouts adapt to different screen sizes
  • Emails send automatically and in order
  • Existing contact preferences are respected
  • The full journey can be tested before launch
  • Useful actions can be measured without sending personal form details into analytics
